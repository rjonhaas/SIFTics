## CRITICAL CORRECTION: OneDrive Transfer Direction

**F-011 was incorrect.** Directional analysis of the PCAP confirms the 36MB transfer involving `oneclient.sfx.ms` (104.119.185.124) was **INBOUND to DESKTOP-SDN1RPT** — OneDrive downloaded 36.3MB TO the victim machine, consistent with OneDrive client auto-update (OneDriveSetup.exe). DESKTOP sent only 0.003MB to OneDrive. No bulk file exfiltration via OneDrive is supported by the evidence. **(F-017 supersedes F-011.)**

Actual exfiltration is via two channels:
- **DCSync** — all C137.local domain hashes including KRBTGT extracted via DRSUAPI protocol at 21:59 UTC Sep 18 (F-003, 196 frames); transmitted through Meterpreter C2 to 203.78.103.109:443
- **RDP clipboard / Meterpreter download** — FileShare\Secret files (≤478b each) consistent with clipboard copy during attacker's DC01 RDP session (11MB RDP display stream to 194.61.24.102, all from DC01:3389); file access confirmed via LNK artifacts (F-014)
